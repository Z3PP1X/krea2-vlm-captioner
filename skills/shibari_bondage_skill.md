# Shibari & Bondage Photoshoot Captioning Skill

This skill provides an exhaustive domain-specific vocabulary and structural guidelines for annotating fine-art shibari, Japanese rope bondage (kinbaku), and high-fashion fetish/restraint photography for generative model training.

## 1. Knot & Rigging Taxonomy

When inspecting rope arrangements, identify and describe the specific structural patterns:

- **Takate-Kote (Box Tie / Upper Arm Binding)**:
  - Description: Classic Japanese upper-body restraint locking the elbows behind the back with horizontal chest wraps and vertical friction lock-offs.
  - Key elements: Stem line running along the spine, chest ropes compressing pectoral tissue, double-column cuffs around upper arms, wrists secured at the small of the back.
- **Hishime (Diamond Chest Harness)**:
  - Description: Geometric diamond-pattern rope weaving across the chest, sternum, and ribcage.
  - Key elements: Clean intersection knots, symmetry across the breastbone, tension lines contouring around the torso.
- **Kikko (Tortoise Shell Harness)**:
  - Description: Hexagonal or interlocking woven pattern enveloping the torso, resembling a turtle shell.
- **Karada (Full Body Harness)**:
  - Description: Continuous rope lines running from shoulders to hips, creating vertical and horizontal grids along the torso.
- **Friction Wraps (Honte) & Hitches**:
  - Description: Half-hitches, munter hitches, and locking loops securing rope tension without metal fasteners.
- **Suspension Mechanics**:
  - *Floor Tie / Static Grounded*: Subject rests kneeling, seated, or prone with limbs locked.
  - *Partial Suspension*: Subject standing on tiptoes or with torso lifted, bearing partial weight on suspension lines.
  - *Full / Inverted Suspension*: Subject completely aloft, weight borne by primary suspension lines attached to ceiling hardpoints or bamboo poles.

## 2. Rope & Hardware Material Physics

Avoid generic words like "ropes" or "chains". Describe material composition, tactile finish, and physical interaction with the body:

- **Rope Fiber & Quality**:
  - `unbleached twisted Japanese hemp rope (asanawa)`: Natural blonde or light tan color, distinct 3-ply twist, crisp fibrous texture.
  - `raw oiled jute rope`: Darker earthy brown, subtle surface fuzz, dense fibrous weave.
  - `smooth spun cotton rope`: Soft, clean white or dyed, flexible drape.
  - `rope diameter`: Typically 5mm to 8mm, showing noticeable physical indentation and biting compression into epidermal flesh.
- **Metallic Hardware & Rigging**:
  - `polished chrome / stainless steel handcuffs`: Highly reflective metallic specular sheen, locking ratchet mechanism, link chain connecting the wrists.
  - `welded heavy steel O-rings and D-rings`: Used at central harness anchor points, reflecting directional studio light.
  - `forged aluminum / steel locking carabiners`: Connecting suspension rope lines to overhead ceiling hardpoints or spreader bars.
  - `spreader bars`: Polished tubular steel or hardwood dowel with eye-bolts keeping ankles or wrists strictly separated.
  - `metal ball gags`: Perforated silicone or chrome sphere secured by a wide black leather strap with silver roller buckle.
- **Leather Restraints**:
  - `matte black saddle leather collars`: Wide neck choker with central nickel O-ring, edge-stitched detailing, and locking buckle.
  - `padded leather wrist and ankle cuffs`: Sheepskin lining, contrast stitching, heavy-duty D-rings with attached padlock or chain.

Describe how the restraints affect anatomy, posture, and muscle tension:
- **Spinal Geometry**: Arched lumbar spine, shoulder blades drawn tightly together, exposed clavicles, stretched neck.
- **Skin Indentations**: Shallow epidermal compression lines where taut ropes bite into thighs, upper arms, or waist, highlighting skin elasticity.

## 4. Photographic Staging & Lighting Aesthetics

In fine-art restraint photography, lighting and environment are critical:
- **Chiaroscuro & Studio Void**: Pitch-black background with high-contrast directional spotlighting creating deep shadows and razor-sharp sculptural highlights on skin contours and rope ridges.
- **Reflective Flooring**: Black high-gloss reflective floor casting mirror-like upside-down specular reflections of the kneeling or suspended subject.
- **Rim & Kicker Lighting**: Narrow rim light skimming along the silhouette, catching the fuzz of hemp fibers and edge of chrome chains.
- **Optics**: Sharp 50mm or 85mm portrait focal length, f/2.8 shallow depth of field, focused squarely on the intricate knot work and subject expression, with gentle falloff into the background.
