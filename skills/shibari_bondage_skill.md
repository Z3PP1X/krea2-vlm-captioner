# Shibari & Bondage Photoshoot Captioning Skill

This skill provides an exhaustive domain-specific vocabulary and structural guidelines for annotating fine-art shibari, Japanese rope bondage (kinbaku), and high-fashion fetish/restraint photography for generative model training (Krea 2).

---

## 1. Model Position & Posture Geometry

When analyzing the subject's anatomy and physical posture:
- **Core Stance**:
  - *Seiza*: Sitting back on heels with shins pressed flat against the floor, upright spine.
  - *High Kneeling*: Kneeling upright with torso vertical, thighs perpendicular to the floor.
  - *All-Fours (Tabletop / Quadruped)*: Kneeling with arched lumbar spine (lordosis), palms or elbows planted on the floor.
  - *Standing / Leaning*: Standing upright, weight shifted onto one hip or balanced on tiptoes; leaning against a suspension pillar or wall.
  - *Prone*: Lying flat face-down, chest touching floor, neck turned sideways.
  - *Supine*: Lying flat on the back, ribcage lifted, limbs anchored outward.
  - *Suspension / Partial Suspension*: Suspended aloft with toes lightly brushing the floor, or fully airborne in an inverted or horizontal suspension plane.
- **Limb Angles & Tension**:
  - Shoulders drawn back with scapulae pressed together, exposing the throat and clavicles.
  - Elbows drawn tightly behind the spine with wrists crossed or overlapping.
  - Legs held together at thighs and calves, or forced wide apart by a spreader bar.
  - Pronounced muscle tension: flexed quadriceps, tensed abdominal core, arched foot arches.

## 2. Bondage Type & Discipline Classification

Classify the overarching style and discipline of restraint:
- **Japanese Rope Bondage (Kinbaku / Shibari)**: Traditional fiber cordage utilizing geometric friction knots and body contouring.
- **Metallic Chain Restraint**: Heavy-gauge welded steel links and high-fashion chrome hardware emphasizing hard reflections and cold industrial contrast.
- **Leather Restraint**: Thick oil-tanned harness leather straps, posture collars, and buckle-locking wrist/ankle cuffs.
- **Suspension / Partial Suspension (Tsuri)**: Dynamic rigging supporting body weight aloft via overhead hardpoints.
- **Predicament Bondage**: Restraint geometry where movement in one direction causes increased tension in another (e.g., frog tie, neck-to-ankle tether).
- **Aesthetic / Decorative Harness**: Elaborate wearable patterns (hishime, kikko) designed for visual symmetry without complete immobilization.

## 3. Bondage Equipment & Material Specifications

Specify exact equipment, gauges, and physical finishes:
- **Rope Fiber & Quality**:
  - `unbleached twisted Japanese hemp rope (asanawa)`: Light tan/blonde color, 3-ply twisted strand construction, crisp surface fuzz.
  - `raw oiled jute cordage`: Earthy brown tone, softened with mineral or camellia oil, dense fiber core.
  - `cordage diameter`: Explicitly designate 5mm, 6mm, or 8mm diameter cords.
- **Metallic Hardware**:
  - `polished chrome / stainless steel handcuffs`: Specular metallic reflections, double-locking mechanism, connecting chain links.
  - `welded heavy steel O-rings and D-rings`: Heavy-duty anchor junctions reflecting directional studio lights.
  - `forged locking carabiners & swivel hitches`: Connecting rope stems to overhead ceiling anchors or floor plates.
  - `spreader bars`: Polished tubular stainless steel or solid wood dowels with welded eye-bolts holding limbs strictly separated.
  - `padlocks & chains`: Brass or chrome keyed padlocks securing chain links.
- **Leather Accessories**:
  - Wide posture collars with front D-ring, nickel roller buckles, leather blindfolds, silicone or chrome metal ball gags.

## 4. Bondage Position & Anatomical Rigging Topology

Detail the exact anatomical placement, routing, and physical interaction:
- **Upper Body Rigging**:
  - *Takate-Kote (Box Tie / Gote)*: Upper arm double-column cuffs, elbows pulled tightly together behind the back, wrists tied at the lumbar spine, central stem line running along vertebrae.
  - *Hishime (Diamond Chest Harness)*: Symmetrical diamond grid crossing the breastbone, wrapping under armpits and across ribs.
  - *Kikko (Tortoise Shell)*: Hexagonal woven pattern contouring the chest and abdominal wall.
- **Lower Body Rigging**:
  - *Frog Tie*: Knees bent outward with ankles bound to the upper thighs or hips.
  - *Thigh & Calf Ties*: Horizontal rope wraps securing legs together or to furniture.
  - *Spreader Bar Rigging*: Ankle cuffs locked to opposite ends of a rigid bar.
- **Physical Contact & Skin Dynamics**:
  - *Skin Indentation Lines*: Deep epidermal compression marks where taut cordage or metal bites into soft flesh of thighs, biceps, and torso.
  - *Tension Reddening*: Authentic erythema and blush along pressure lines highlighting natural body elasticity.

## 5. Photographic Staging & Lighting Aesthetics

- **Chiaroscuro & Studio Void**: High-contrast directional lighting against a pitch-black background, carving razor-sharp highlights along skin and hardware.
- **Glossy Reflective Flooring**: Black high-gloss floor showing crisp inverted specular reflections of the subject and hardware.
- **Optics**: 50mm or 85mm portrait focal length, f/2.8 shallow depth of field, sharp foreground plane focused on knot/hardware tension with smooth background falloff.
